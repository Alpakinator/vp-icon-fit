Vox Populi icon fit: every changed icon, before and after

Each card shows one icon. In game draws it exactly as the game does: the ring frame first, the icon on top at offset 0,0, at its real pixel size, next to a magnified copy with hard pixels. The magnified copy carries the guides: crosshair through the standard centre, the standard and the measured outline, and for font icons the 1 px margin in blue (switch them off with Guides off). In atlas shows the raw atlas cell on a checkerboard instead. Click a card to see every size of that icon at once.

Flip swaps before and after in place, which shows a half-pixel move far better than side by side. Images are the actual atlas pixels (decoded from the DXT/BGRA files), stored losslessly.
